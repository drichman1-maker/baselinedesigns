Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

i8 Designs ("i8 Designs," "we," "us," or "I") is an independent design studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit design.i8cap.com (the "Site") or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Information you give us directly

If you reach out through our contact form or by email, you choose what to share. This typically includes your name, email address, an optional company or project name, your project type, budget range, timeline, and any details you include in your message.

When you submit our contact form, your details are sent through Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service, which delivers them to us by email. We receive the name, email address, and any project details you choose to provide, and we use that information only to respond to your inquiry. See Web3Forms' own privacy practices at web3forms.com/privacy.

Information collected automatically

The Site is a static website. We do not run our own analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. However, the Site is delivered through third-party infrastructure (see below), and those providers may automatically log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, as part of delivering and securing the Site.

Third-Party Services

To keep the Site fast and lightweight, we load resources from, and rely on, third-party services. When your browser requests these resources, the relevant provider may receive your IP address and request information under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the Site.
  • Google Fonts, which provides the web fonts used across the Site.
  • Content delivery networks such as unpkg and Cloudflare-hosted CDNs, which serve styling and interface scripts.
  • Your email provider, when you use the contact form or email us directly.

We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their policies.

How We Use Information

We use the information you send us only to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about a potential or active project;
  • Prepare proposals, scope work, and deliver services you've engaged us for; and
  • Keep records of our correspondence and engagements.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How We Share Information

We do not share your personal information except in limited cases: with trusted specialists or contractors brought in to help deliver a project (and only as needed for that work), with service providers that operate our infrastructure (such as our email and hosting providers), or where required by law.

Data Retention

We keep correspondence and project information for as long as needed to respond to you, deliver and maintain our services, and meet legal, accounting, or recordkeeping obligations. You may ask us to delete information you've sent us, and we'll honor that request where we're not required to retain it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain uses. To exercise any of these rights, email us at douglasrichman1@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for business clients and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you send us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page; your continued use of the Site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information? Email douglasrichman1@gmail.com.
